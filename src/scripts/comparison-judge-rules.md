# Judging comparison pairs — rules

A Greek price-comparison app shows two supermarket chains' listings side by side as THE SAME product, so a shopper can compare their prices. For each pair of listing names (A and B) decide whether they are the SAME sellable item.

A wrong «same» shows a shopper a false price comparison — that is the worst outcome. A wrong «unsure» only hides a correct comparison. When in doubt, choose «unsure».

## Step 1 — find the words that appear in only one name

Ignore capital letters, accents, word order, punctuation, and the same word written in Greek or English (Vanilla = Βανίλια, Lemon = Λεμόνι, Medium = Μέτρια, Light = Ελαφρύ, Dark = Σκούρο, XL = Extra Large).

## Step 2 — mark each one-sided word

**OMITTED** — something the other chain simply did not write:
- the product type (Βαφή Μαλλιών, Σερβιέτες, Αφρόλουτρο, Υγρό Πιάτων, Ταμπόν, Λακ, Μάσκα Προσώπου)
- the company written before the brand («Nestle Nescafe» = «Nescafe», «Schwarzkopf Taft» = «Taft»)
- a claim or description (Αντιβακτηριδιακό, 24ωρη προστασία, με Ενεργό Χλώριο, Κατεψυγμένο, σε Φάκελο, Με Άρωμα, Multipack, Premium Blend, Dark Roast, Αλεσμένος)
- the ingredients spelled out, the origin
- the size, weight or count, when the other name states no DIFFERENT one
- a size label that restates a number the other name gives (nappies: Junior = No5, Maxi = No4, X-Large = No6)

Counts written differently are the same count when the number matches: 8τεμ = 8άρι = 8 τεμάχια; κάψουλες / caps / tabs / pods / μεζούρες (μεζ, Mεζ, mez) / πλύσεις with the same number are the same pack. 760g = 0,760kg, 1,5lt = 1500ml, gr = g = γρ.

**VARIANT** — a word that says WHICH product of the brand it is: flavour, scent, colour, shade or model number, grain or flour (Σίκαλης = rye, Σίτου = wheat, Ολικής = wholemeal), fat %, age range, or an edition the brand also sells without it (Ultra, Sensitive, Max, Pro, Plus, Light, Zero, 3σε1, Exotic).

## Step 3 — decide

- **different** — the brands differ, or both names state the same kind of detail with different values (rye vs wheat, two different scents, 2 vs 5 refills, shade 5/0 vs 6/0, 21 vs 40 capsules, different sizes).
- **unsure** — a VARIANT word appears in one name only, or one name has no brand at all, or you cannot tell.
- **same** — every one-sided word is OMITTED and nothing conflicts.

Judge every pair on its own, only from the two names. Do not look anything up.
